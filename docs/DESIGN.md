# Design System Specification

## Visual Identity & Color Philosophy
The design system utilizes a professional and grounded color palette characterized by muted slate tones and high-contrast accents. 

- **Primary Brand Color**: A sophisticated slate blue (#565e74) used for core structural elements and primary navigation.
- **Secondary Accent**: A vibrant, high-energy royal blue (#4e45e4) reserved for key calls to action and interactive highlights.
- **Tertiary & Neutral Tones**: A range of cool greys (#526075 and #71717a) provide depth and clarity to backgrounds and non-interactive surfaces.

The color mode is strictly **Light**, ensuring maximum legibility and a clean, traditional application interface.

## Typography
The system employs **Inter** as the universal typeface for headlines, body copy, and labels. This choice ensures:
- Exceptional readability across all screen sizes.
- A modern, neutral character that allows content to take center stage.
- Visual consistency across the entire user journey.

## Layout & Spatial Rhythm
The spatial system is configured for a **Normal** density (level 2). This provides a balanced, breathable layout that avoids the cramped nature of utility-heavy apps while maintaining a high information density suitable for professional productivity.

## Shape & Geometry
The interface utilizes a **Subtle** corner treatment (roundedness level 1). This slight softening of edges maintains a professional, precise, and organized aesthetic while feeling more modern than strictly sharp corners. It communicates stability and reliability.
